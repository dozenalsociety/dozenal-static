Dozenal Pi Day 11E9 (2013.)

Happy Dozenal Pi Day (π Day) from the Dozenal Society of America!

Many people are familiar with "Pi Day" as March 14d, which when written in numbers only becomes "3-14d," which are the first three digits of π in the decimal system: 3.14159625...d. Well, in dozenal π is 3.18480949...z that means that March 18z, or 3-18z, is Dozenal Pi Day. In decimal, that date is March 20d; or today. So happy Pi Day!

π is a natural constant which has traditionally been defined as the ratio of the diameter of a circle to its circumference. It can also be defined in many other ways; probably the most useful is “the number of radians in an about-face.” This is the greatest possible change in direction (turning further than that is really just a smaller angle turning the other way), and makes possible some other notation and mathematical tricks which are beyond the scope of this little piece.

Using π in dozenal has some important advantages over using it in decimal. Most importantly, dozenal π is more accurate than decimal π for the same number of digits of estimation. Since π is a non-terminating fraction, it’s impossible to state it with exact accuracy; however, it is sometimes possible to state it with more accuracy. Dozenal makes this much easier; the table below shows just how much easier (inaccuracies are listed in pergross, and are themselves necessarily rounded:

Dozenal Decimal
π = 3.18480949z π = 3.14159265d
3.18z 0.48%z 3.14d 0.29%z
3.185z 0.03B%z 3.142d 0.085%z
3.1848z 0.0009%z 3.1416d 0.001A%z

The table shows that decimal π is actually more accurate than dozenal π to two places. But to three and four places, dozenal π clearly runs away with the prize. Indeed, the three-digit rounding is more than twice as accurate in dozenal than in decimal; and the four-digit rounding is an entire order of magnitude more accurate. It's hard to argue with results like this.

So enjoy dozenal π day; use it to spread the glories of dozenal over the pretensions of the decimal π day six days ago. Dozenal makes arithmetic better; π provides yet another example of base twelve’s greatness.
