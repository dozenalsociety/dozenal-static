Interactive Dozenal Compass Rose

Sources: Dozenal Compass Directions thread at DozensOnline forum, and accompanying spreadsheet. See the thread for explanations of any unfamiliar terminology. Choose a division of the compass. Hover over, click, or focus a direction to display its full name and angular/time equivalents in the center; or use Tab, Shift + Tab, Right Arrow, or Left Arrow to cycle through compass directions. Dozenal numbers are annotated with a subscript z; decimal numbers with a subscript d.
