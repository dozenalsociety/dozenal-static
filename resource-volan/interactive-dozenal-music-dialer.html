Interactive Dozenal Chord Dialer

This application has two modes: The Interactive Dozenal Chord Dialer is accessed via the button to the left of the title. The Interactive Dozenal Scale Dialer is accessed via the button to the right of the title.

Musical note-numbers are expressed as dozenal doublings of frequency with respect to C in octave 0 (C0). In other words, each note number is the binary logarithm of the ratio of its frequency to the frequency of C0, expressed in base twelve.

The integer digit (whole doubling) encodes the octave number. The first fractional digit (uncia·doubling) encodes the semitone (when rounded). The second and third fractional digits (bicia·doubling and tricia·doubling) are the dozenal equivalents of “dimes” and “cents”.

Negative octaves are indicated by a line over the whole doubling. However, the fractional digits are always positive.

Middle C = 4.0z doublings. High C = 5.0z doublings. Low C = 3.0z doublings. Supported notes range from C1 to B↊ (from 1.0z to ↊.↋z doublings). This covers the MIDI range, plus an octave beyond.

The polygon inside the dial depicts the chord or scale. The root note of the chord or scale is highlighed on the dial. You can rotate the dial, or rotate the polygon. This is governed by the mode buttons flanking the corners of the dial.

In the upper left corner of the dial is the Rotate Polygon mode button. When it is select, the dial remains fixed and you can grab and drag the polygon to rotate it. Each complete clockwise turn doubles the frequencies. Counterclockwise halves them.

In the upper right corner of the dial is the Rotate Dial mode button. When it is selected, the polygon remains fixed and you can grab and drag the dial to rotate it. Each complete counterclockwise turn doubles the frequencies. Clockwise halves them.

In either rotation mode, the shape of the polygon adjusts slightly to the positions of the notes, given their offsets from equal temperament.

The keyboard below also depicts the chord or scale. To pick a new root note, select any key on the keyboard, or use ← or →.

In Chord mode, use the Arpeggio Mode button beside Play Chord to play the chord notes plus the upper octave of the root in ascending and descending sequence, with a beat of rest between directions. This button is unavailable for Unison. Press ▶️ to start playing the chord or scale. Press ⏹️ to stop. A scale plays first ascending, then descending, then repeats, with a beat of rest between directions. The tempo is one beat per “beat” (three twinklings) or 40z prime·tricia·frequenciels (0.96d Hz or 57.6d BPM).

You can adjust settings during playback. Playback continues indefinitely as long as you continue interacting with the Dialer. Otherwise, playback times out one trice (50d seconds) after last user input.
